Simulation

Particle on a ring

A free particle on a ring threaded by Aharonov–Bohm flux. Instead of coloring the wavefunction's phase, the sim makes it a second coordinate, so ψ(θ) traces a loop on a torus. As it evolves, Talbot revivals (the packet splitting into copies and reassembling) weave a carpet across the surface. A Möbius-strip version is next.

JavaScript · canvas
